# Styling intro

One of my favorite things to do in the frontend practice is to give some character to the stuff I build through the use of certain colors available from a palette, for example. Or the spacing between certain parts of the components I build. The guidelines usually come from people who know what they are doing, also known as designers. Lately there is a surge in the importance we give to the so called micro-interactions, or the small animations that happen when a user interacts with our components. These are now more than ever an indication of the care and attention the developer has put into the product if you asked me. Which, I guess you didn't.

Throughout this section, we'll learn how Vue enables us to style our components and my hope is that you will see how powerful and flexile the framework really is when it comes to styling.
