# Challenge!

::challenge
Make this level meter work
::

In this challenge you'll put your conditional rendering skills to the test.

In `src/App.vue` there are three messages, but they are all shown at the same time. Your job is to make the page show only **one** of them, depending on the current `level`:

- If the `level` is below `1`, show the first message.
- If the `level` is below `3`, show the second message.
- Otherwise, show the third message.

Use the `v-if`, `v-else-if` and `v-else` directives to chain these conditions together, just like you did in the previous lesson.

When you are done, use the **Check my work** button below to verify your solution.
